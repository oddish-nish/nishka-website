What if you could turn your living room into a chaotic kitchen where you and your friends frantically cook together? That's Order's Up. The Tired Research Assistant's Assistant

hack-use by cua
3rd Place

hack-use by cua
3rd Place

I've been that undergrad research assistant stuck doing grunt work. I built a scraper that worked perfectly—until a minor UI update broke everything. The science hadn't changed, but the tooling collapsed. This project was born from that frustration. What if we built something that sees the screen like a human would, something that adapts when things change? The goal isn't to replace scientific thinking—it's to let researchers actually do research. Computer-Using Agent (CUA) that automates research assistant tasks by interacting with scientific websites like a human researcher. Navigates RCSB Protein Data Bank, extracts gene metadata, and writes structured CSV files. Robust to UI changes through visual reasoning rather than hardcoded selectors. Includes Streamlit dashboard for data visualization.

📝 Finalist

Rabbit Hole

Google AI Studio Competition on Kaggle

Google AI Studio Competition on Kaggle

Learning used to be exciting. One question could open an entire world, but somewhere along the way we stopped exploring and started accepting quick answers. Rabbit Hole brings back that spark. Instead of giving a direct answer, it turns any question into a guided journey—an expanding map of connected ideas, misconceptions, real-life examples, and simple experiments you can try yourself. Built entirely with Gemini 3 Pro, designed to help people think, question, and understand through curiosity again. Curiosity-first learning tool powered by Gemini 3 Pro. Transforms questions into guided journeys with expanding maps of connected ideas, misconceptions, examples, and experiments. Uses Gemini's multimodality for adaptive explanations (diagrams, visuals, stories, analogies, audio). Features personalized exploration, bookmarking, understanding tests, and dark mode. Built entirely in Google AI Studio.
